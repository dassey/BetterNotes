Install on iPad / iPhone

Open this page in Safari → tap the Share button → Add to Home Screen. BetterNotes then runs full-screen and fully offline, like a native app.

Writing & drawing

  • Infinite canvas — new notes grow in every direction; just scroll and keep writing. Prefer a classic sheet? Switch any note between Infinite and Page in its paper menu (the 📄 button).
  • Pen / Highlighter — tap the active tool again for colors and sizes. Apple Pencil pressure shapes the ink.
  • Lines — a long, nearly straight stroke straightens itself on lift and snaps to 15° steps (Settings → Auto-straighten). Draw a corner sharply and the stabilizer keeps it sharp.
  • Shaky hands? — raise the Steady slider (in the pen popover, or “Stabilize strokes” in Settings). It absorbs hand tremor as you write; at high values the ink deliberately trails the pen a touch.
  • Shapes — draw a line, box, circle or triangle and keep the pen pressed down and still at the end; after a moment the shape snaps perfect (a colored glow confirms it), then lift. Lifting too early skips the snap. Hold time is adjustable in Settings.
  • Saved pens — the floating bar under the toolbar holds your favorite pens. Tap a chip to switch, + to save the current color/size, and tap the active chip again to update or remove it. Hide the bar in Settings if you want the space.
  • Palm rejection — automatic: the first time this device sees an Apple Pencil, fingers switch to scrolling and zooming while only the Pencil draws. Change it under Settings → Fingers. Even when fingers may draw, a touch right after Pencil activity just scrolls.
  • Eraser — Precise mode erases just what’s under it (splitting strokes); tap the eraser tool again to switch modes or resize.
  • Two-finger tap — undo. Three-finger tap — redo.

Selecting & tidying

  • Select — circle any ink, text or images (or tap the select tool a second time to switch to rectangle drag), then drag to move, use the corner handles to resize, or tap Tidy to smooth out messy handwriting.
  • Copy / Delete / Color — from the bar above the selection. A single tap picks one item; tap empty space to clear the selection.
  • Hand tool — pick it when you want nothing armed: every finger or pen touch just pans, nothing draws or selects.
  • Read as text — select handwriting and tap Read: the app shows it as an image that iPadOS can read with a touch-and-hold (Live Text). Copy the words, then Paste as text box.
  • Laser pointer — a glowing, fading trail for pointing things out while presenting or studying together; it's never saved.
  • Paper — lined, grid, dots, Cornell (cue column + summary band) and music staff, per note from the paper menu.

Text & Scribble

With the Text tool, tap anywhere to place a text box. On iPad you can handwrite straight into a text box with the Pencil — iPadOS Scribble converts it to typed text on the device. That’s the cleanest “messy handwriting → neat text” path, and it never leaves your iPad.

Recording a lecture

Tap the microphone, then write as usual — everything you write while recording is time-stamped to the audio. Later, press play: your notes dim and reappear in sync, and tapping any word with the hand tool jumps the recording to the moment you wrote it. Recordings live inside the note on your device, never uploaded.

Speech to text. The first time you tap record, BetterNotes offers to download an open speech model (once; 50 to 300 MB depending on the size you pick in Settings), then reloads the page a single time to switch on fast multithreaded recognition. Say yes and what is said shows up as live captions and is kept as a transcript next to the player: tap a passage to jump the audio there, copy it, search for it from the home screen, and it travels along in the PDF export. Everything runs on this device; nothing is uploaded. Older recordings can be transcribed later from the transcript panel, and you can change your mind any time under Settings → Audio recording.

Photos

Tap the photo button to insert images; drag to move. Corner handles resize keeping proportions; side handles stretch one direction freely. Select a photo and tap Crop to trim it — the crop is non-destructive, so cropping again can bring the edges back. (A selected text box gets side handles too, for its wrap width.) Images are stored inside the note on your device.

Folders & printing

Use the folder chips on the home screen to sort notes by subject (tap an active chip to rename or delete it); a note's ⋯ menu moves it. Share → Export PDF writes a real vector PDF (crisp at any zoom, tiny file: ink as true paths, photos embedded, text selectable) with no extra software; Print remains as a fallback through the system print sheet.

Privacy

No accounts. No tracking. No uploads. Notes live in your browser’s on-device storage — use Settings → “Back up all notes” to save them to a file, and “Protect storage” to ask the device to keep them safe. If you ever clear Safari website data, backed-up files are your safety net.

Read as text

On iPad or iPhone, touch and hold the handwriting below — the device reads it on-device (Live Text). Select the words, tap Copy, then press the button to drop them into your note as typed text.

Nothing leaves your device — recognition is done by iPadOS itself.
